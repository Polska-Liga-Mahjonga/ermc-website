    The 7th European Riichi Mahjong Championship comes to Katowice, Poland, on 6–8 August 2027.

    With 200 players from across Europe (and beyond), it will be the biggest ERMC ever: three days and 14 hanchans of riichi mahjong at the International Congress Centre, in the heart of Katowice and right next to the iconic Spodek arena.

    • When: 6–8 August 2027, with the referee seminar and EMA Grand Assembly on 5 August
    • Where: International Congress Centre, Katowice
    • Players: 200
    • Format: 14 hanchans over three days (agenda)
    • Registration: opens 1 February 2027
