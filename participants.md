ERMC 2027 is played by delegations from the member countries of the
[European Mahjong Association (EMA)](https://mahjong-europe.org/), together with a number of
invited players from outside Europe. Each EMA country has a quota of places, and its national
mahjong organisation decides who plays in its delegation.

**Official country quotas will be posted at the
[EMA quota page](http://mahjong-europe.org/ranking/quotas_RCR.html).**

The list of participants will be published here once
[registration]({{ '/registration/' | relative_url }}) opens.
