Registration will open on **February 1st, 2027**.

## Registration fee

The registration fee is **€200**. It includes:

- **Lunch on all three tournament days**, served in a separate room at the venue.
- **Drinks and snacks throughout the day:** water and juice from dispensers, coffee from
  an espresso machine, a choice of teas, and cakes and cookies at every coffee break,
  refilled by the venue staff.
- **A goodie bag** for every participant.
- **Prizes** for the top 10 players and for special achievements. Details will be
  announced later.

## Before you register

Places at ERMC 2027 are given out through the EMA country quotas (see
[Participants]({{ '/participants/' | relative_url }})), and each national mahjong organisation
chooses its own delegation.

**Please make sure your national organisation has sent us its official delegation, with you
on it, before you send your registration.** If you are not sure whether you are in your
country's delegation, ask your national organisation first.

Questions about registration? [Contact us]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).
