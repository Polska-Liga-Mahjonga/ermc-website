ERMC 2027 takes place in the [International Congress Centre in Katowice](https://www.mckkatowice.pl/en/)
(Międzynarodowe Centrum Kongresowe, MCK), right in the city centre.

**Address:** plac Sławika i Antalla 1, 40-163 Katowice ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mi%C4%99dzynarodowe+Centrum+Kongresowe+Katowice))

## The building

Opened in 2015, MCK is one of the largest congress centres in Poland. It stands in the
Katowice Culture Zone, a former coal-mine site that is now home to the city's main
cultural landmarks: the flying-saucer-shaped Spodek arena, the Silesian Museum and the
concert hall of the Polish National Radio Symphony Orchestra. A canyon cuts through the
middle of the building to keep the view of Spodek open, and the two are also linked by an
underground passage.

## Where we play

We will play in **Ballroom B** (Sala Balowa B), a 400 m² hall. There will also be a
separate break area, and meals will be served in another hall nearby, so the playing
room stays quiet.

## Food and drink

Catering at MCK is provided by the venue's own partner caterers, who regularly serve
large international events and can prepare meals for a wide range of diets, including
plenty of meat-free options. Food and drink are also sold at the venue during events.
Details of meals during the championship will be announced closer to the date.
